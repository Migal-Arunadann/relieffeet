Why reflexology

Your feet are a map of your whole body.

Reflexology applies precise pressure to zones on the feet that correspond to organs, nerves and joints. At Relief Feet, every session begins with a plain-language check-up of your history — then hands do the rest.

We don't just rub feet. Each package pairs reflexology with the right adjunct — cupping, targeted pressure-point work or lifestyle guidance. Patients regularly tell us they were asked how they eat and sleep before being handed a treatment plan.

How a visit goes

Four unhurried steps. Zero paperwork.

01

Walk in — no booking needed

We're open around the clock, so 6 a.m. stiffness and midnight aches are equally welcome. Tell us at the door what hurts.

02

Read-out & zone mapping

The therapist presses through foot zones to find where your body is holding tension, and explains what they feel in plain terms.

03

The session

Slow, deliberate reflexology — often combined with cupping or focused therapy for your condition. Clean linens, calm room, courteous staff.

04

Plan & aftercare

You leave with a suggested number of sittings, food and habit tweaks, and a phone you can actually call between visits.
