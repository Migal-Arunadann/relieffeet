Before you walk in

Questions people actually ask at the door

Does reflexology hurt?

There can be tenderness where your body holds tension — it should feel like "good hurt", never bruising pain. Tell your therapist anytime and the pressure changes immediately.

How many sessions will I need?

Most visitors see meaningful change in 3–5 sittings; long-standing issues take more. Your therapist gives an honest estimate after the first read-out — we'd rather set real expectations than promise miracles.

Is it a replacement for my doctor?

No. Reflexology is complementary therapy. For kidney stones, diabetes, ligament tears or anything under active medical care, keep following your doctor — we work alongside that plan, not against it.

Do you have a female therapist?

Yes — Ms. Mahalakshmi handles women's wellness, periods-related concerns and general reflexology, and is highly recommended in our reviews.

Really open 24 hours?

Yes, all seven days. If the door is shut at odd hours, call 070104 74579 and someone will attend to you.
